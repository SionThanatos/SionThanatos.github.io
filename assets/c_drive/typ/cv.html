I am currently a Ph.D. student at the Department of Computing, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU), where I am advised by Prof. Daniel Xiapu Luo. Prior to joining PolyU, I obtained my B.Eng. degree from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST) in 2024, where I was advised by Prof. Haoyu Wang.

My research focuses on System Security, Windows Internals, and Vulnerability Discovery, and I maintain a close research collaboration with and receive guidance from Prof. Zhiniang Peng. Beyond academia, I work as an independent vulnerability researcher, exploring technically compelling real-world vulnerabilities.

My work has uncovered hundreds of real-world vulnerabilities across the Windows, Linux, and macOS ecosystems, earning me recognition as a Microsoft Security Response Center (MSRC) Most Valuable Researcher (MVR). I have appeared on the MSRC Researcher Leaderboard for multiple consecutive quarters, ranking 27th globally on the 2026 MVR leaderboard and 9th on the Windows leaderboard. In the MSRC 2026 Q2 Security Researcher Leaderboard, I ranked 4th overall and 1st on the Windows leaderboard.
